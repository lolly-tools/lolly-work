Open **https://__DOMAIN__/admin** and sign in with your YunoHost account (the portal signs you in; the first owner is `__ADMIN__`). The web app itself is at **https://__DOMAIN__/**.

Three places to look first:

- **People** in the console: everyone who has signed in, with role and groups as YunoHost provides them.
- **Users › Groups and permissions** in the YunoHost admin: give people the `admin`, `approver` or `author` permission of this app to change their Lolly Work role; put them in YunoHost groups to target them with tool overlays and approval chains.
- **This app's config panel** for the access mode, guest links and telemetry.

The instance starts with a neutral starter brand and the whole community tool set. To make it yours, see the admin documentation (in the console at `/admin#/docs`): import your design tokens, or point the instance pack at your own brand pack.
